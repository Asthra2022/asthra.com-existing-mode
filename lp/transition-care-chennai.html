A few questions you might have.

What is transition care, and how does Asthra help?

Transition care is supervised recovery support between hospital discharge and going home. Asthra provides doctor-led plans, 24/7 nursing, rehab and family guidance.

Who can benefit from Asthra’s transition care?

It suits seniors and adults recovering from surgery, stroke, fractures, cardiac surgeries or a long illness, especially when the family can’t provide round-the-clock care at home.

How does Asthra help with post-surgery recovery?

Asthra supports patients with trained nursing, wound dressing, mobility and physiotherapy support, diet and medication supervision, and family guidance. This follows the surgeon’s discharge plan.

Do you provide physiotherapy after surgery?

Yes, guided physiotherapy for strength, balance and mobility is a core part of recovery after knee, hip, spine, shoulder and cardiac surgery.

Can I book short-term care for just a few days ?

Yes, Asthra offers short-term care for a few days, with the option to extend based on the elder’s recovery.

What is the cost of post-surgery care at Asthra ?

Every recovery is different, so Asthra prepares a personalised quote based on the patient’s condition, the care required and the expected duration. Share the surgery details and discharge date to get an estimate.

How is transition care different from regular home nursing

Home nursing usually covers routine tasks like injections or dressings. Transition care is a planned program that starts at hospital discharge and combines nursing, therapy, medication review, doctor coordination, and follow-up, with clear recovery goals and an end point.

Can you handle knee and hip replacement rehabilitation?

Yes. This includes pain control, walking with support, guided physiotherapy, swelling management and fall prevention.

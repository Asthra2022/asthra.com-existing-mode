Questions About Palliative Care

What is palliative care for seniors?

Palliative care focuses on comfort, symptom relief and quality of life for people living with serious illness. It can be provided alongside disease-directed treatment.

Does Asthra provide pain and symptom management?

Our team supports symptom observation, prescribed medication assistance and coordination with treating doctors for pain and other distressing symptoms.

Is cancer pain relief support available?

We can discuss nursing support and clinician-directed cancer pain management plans. Prescription changes and specialist interventions require a qualified treating clinician.

Do you offer wound management and pressure sore care?

Care may include skin monitoring, prescribed wound dressings, hygiene and pressure-injury prevention, subject to assessment and staff capabilities.

Can Asthra care for bedridden patients?

Our team can assess needs such as repositioning, feeding assistance, hygiene and nursing support before confirming a suitable care arrangement.

Is family counseling part of palliative care?

Families can discuss emotional support, care decisions and communication needs with the team. Specialist psychological counseling should be confirmed separately.

Is 24/7 nursing support available?

Asthra offers round-the-clock nursing support. Please confirm the level of monitoring and clinical care required for the individual.

Can family members visit?

Family connection is important. Contact the chosen facility to discuss visiting arrangements and video calls.

How much does palliative care cost in Chennai?

Pricing varies with room type, location, nursing requirements and the level of care. Request an individual estimate.

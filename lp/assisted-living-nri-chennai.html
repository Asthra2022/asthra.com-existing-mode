A few questions you might have.

Can I arrange assisted living for my parents while living abroad?

Yes. You can contact our Chennai team by phone or WhatsApp, discuss your parent’s needs and coordinate a visit with a local relative. Ask about a video tour if you cannot travel.

How will I know how my parents are doing?

Our team shares daily health and wellbeing updates with families via WhatsApp. You can discuss preferred contact arrangements during admission.

Is nursing support available at night?

Asthra offers 24/7 nursing and caregiver support, including overnight assistance. The individual care plan depends on your parent’s needs.

Can my parents speak to me on video calls?

Yes. Wi-Fi and support for family video calls help residents stay connected with loved ones overseas.

What if my parent has dementia or memory loss?

Ask about our Mind & Memory Care service. Our team can discuss your parent’s needs and whether an appropriate care arrangement is available.

What does assisted living cost in Chennai?

The displayed rate is a starting price. Final charges vary with room type, location and care requirements. Request a personalised written quotation and confirm inclusions.

Can a family member visit before admission?

Yes. A relative in Chennai can arrange a facility visit, meet the care team and review the accommodation before you decide.

What if my parent needs more care later?

Asthra also offers Transition Care, Rehabilitation Care and Palliative Care. The team can review care requirements and discuss suitable options as needs change.

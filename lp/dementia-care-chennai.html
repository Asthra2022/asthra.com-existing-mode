Questions About Mind & Memory Care

What is Mind & Memory Care for seniors?

It is personalized daily living support for seniors experiencing dementia, memory loss or cognitive changes, with nursing assistance, familiar routines and meaningful activities.

Does Asthra provide care for Alzheimer’s and dementia patients?

Yes Asthra do, Our team can discuss suitable dementia and Alzheimer’s care arrangements. Admission depends on an assessment of individual needs and the facility’s capabilities.

Can you care for seniors with Parkinson’s dementia?

Yes we do, Support may include mobility assistance, personal care, medication routines and daily activities. Please arrange an assessment to confirm suitability.

What if my parent experiences aggression, wandering or sundowning?

These symptoms may require additional supervision and medical assessment. Our team can discuss whether the facility can safely support your parent’s needs.

Do you provide care for elderly depression or late-onset psychosis?

These conditions require assessment and treatment by qualified clinicians. Contact our team to discuss suitable supportive living and care coordination.

Is 24/7 nursing support available?

Asthra offers round-the-clock nursing support. Please confirm the level of supervision and medical support needed for your parent.

Can family members visit their loved ones?

Yes you can, Family connection is important. Contact the facility to discuss visiting arrangements and video calls.

How much does dementia care cost in Chennai?

Pricing depends on room choice, location and individual care requirements. Request a personalized quotation from our team.

How do I arrange admission for my parent?

Call our care team, share your parent’s needs, arrange an assessment and visit the facility before confirming admission.

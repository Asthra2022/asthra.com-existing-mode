A few questions you might have.

How much does assisted living cost at Asthra?

Assisted living at Asthra starts from ₹55,000 per month onwards. The final cost depends on the location, room type, and your parent’s care needs. The monthly fee covers nutritious meals, medication assistance, wellness checks, laundry and housekeeping, comfortable rooms, Wi-Fi.

Can my parent do a trial stay at Asthra?

Yes, Before choosing a long-term stay, Asthra offers trial stays so your parent can experience the atmosphere, food, and level of care. For a few days, weeks, or months, trial stays are offered with the same activities, meals, and care as long-term residents.

Are CCTV monitoring and emergency assistance facilities available?

Yes. Asthra is monitored by CCTV in common areas for resident safety, and emergency call buttons are available so residents can reach caregivers quickly. Trained staff are on duty round the clock to respond to any emergency.

Does Asthra provide 24/7 nursing care, including night shifts?

Yes. Asthra has trained nursing staff on duty round the clock, including night shifts. Nurses monitor residents’ health, assist with medication, and respond quickly to any emergency, so your parent is always safe and cared for.

Are physiotherapy and recreational activities available?

Yes. Asthra offers trained physiotherapy for mobility, balance, and recovery, along with daily recreational activities such as yoga, music, games, and festival celebrations. Everything is adapted to each resident’s comfort and ability.

Are private rooms available for seniors?

Asthra offers private rooms and other room options to suit different needs and budgets. Rooms are clean, furnished, and comfortable, with caregivers always nearby. Pricing depends on location and room type.

Can couples stay together at Asthra?

Yes couples can share a room at Asthra and receive the same care and amenities. Pricing depends on location and room type.

A few questions you might have.

What is assisted living at Asthra?

Assisted living combines a comfortable place to live with help for everyday needs, personal care and companionship. Talk to our team about the right support for your parent.

What types of care are available?

Our services include assisted living, memory care, transition care, rehabilitation, palliative care and care at home. Our team can help you explore suitable options.

Are single and shared rooms available?

Contact our team to discuss current room options and availability at your preferred location.

Can family members visit?

We welcome family involvement. Speak with the team at your chosen home to plan a visit and learn about visiting arrangements.

Can you accommodate dietary preferences?

Tell us about your parent’s dietary preferences and medical needs so our team can discuss suitable meal arrangements.

How do I book a visit?

Use the Book a Visit button or send us a callback request. We will help you choose a home and arrange a convenient time.

How much does care cost?

Costs depend on the location, accommodation and care required. Contact us for a personalised discussion and current pricing.

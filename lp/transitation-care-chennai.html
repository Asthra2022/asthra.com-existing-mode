FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About Transition Care? We're Here to Help.

What is Transition Care for elderly patients?

Transition Care is short-term support for seniors who are medically ready to leave hospital but still need nursing assistance, rehabilitation or help with daily activities.

Who needs post-hospitalisation care in Chennai?

Seniors recovering from surgery, fractures, illness or reduced mobility may benefit. Admission depends on medical stability and the level of support needed.

Does Asthra provide 24/7 nursing care after surgery?

Asthra provides round-the-clock nursing support, medication assistance, vital monitoring and personal care. Confirm specific clinical requirements before admission.

Is physiotherapy available during Transition Care?

Physiotherapy support can be arranged based on the patient's condition and treating doctor's recommendations. Confirm frequency and fees with the care team.

Can elderly patients stay temporarily after discharge?

Yes. Short-term stays are possible subject to assessment and room availability.

What conditions are suitable for elderly recovery care?

Common examples include post-operative recovery, fracture rehabilitation and weakness following illness. Each admission is individually reviewed.

How much does Transition Care cost in Chennai?

Pricing varies by room, nursing needs, length of stay and extra services. Request a personalised quote for accurate inclusions and charges.

Can family members visit during the recovery stay?

Family involvement is encouraged. Confirm visiting hours with the selected facility.

How can I arrange admission after hospital discharge?

Contact the team with the expected discharge date, discharge summary, medication list and care needs to discuss suitability and availability.

Is food and diet customised for medical conditions?

Discuss prescribed dietary needs with the care team in advance to confirm what the facility can safely provide.

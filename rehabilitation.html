Rehabilitation Care Services in Chennai

Comprehensive Rehabilitation Care

Rehabilitation care is designed to help seniors regain physical strength, functional mobility, and daily independence following major surgeries, acute illnesses, neurological events, or prolonged hospital stays.

At Asthra Senior Living Home, our rehabilitation services combine expert medical supervision, customized physical therapy, and compassionate personal support delivered either at our specialized facility or in the comfort of home in Chennai.

Our multidisciplinary team addresses a full spectrum of recovery needs, from post-operative joint strengthening and mobility training to speech therapy, pain management, and functional independence training.

We provide structured, goal-driven recovery programs tailored to individual health conditions to ensure a smooth transition from hospital to active daily life.

Our Specialized Rehabilitation Programs:

  • Post-Orthopedic & Joint Surgery Rehabilitation
  • Stroke & Neurological Recovery Care
  • Cardiopulmonary & Endurance Rehabilitation
  • Geriatric Mobility & Fall Prevention Therapy
  • Post-Hospitalization Transitional Physical Therapy


Post-Orthopedic & Joint Surgery Recovery

  • Seniors recovering from total knee replacement, hip replacement, fractures, or spinal procedures require intensive, step-by-step physical therapy to safely rebuild strength and regain full joint range of motion.
  • Our certified physiotherapists work directly with each resident through guided gait training, therapeutic exercises, and swelling reduction techniques to ensure safe, rapid recovery.


Stroke & Neurological Recovery

  • Neurological events such as strokes, Parkinson’s disease progression, or brain trauma often impair motor skills, balance, and communication abilities.
  • Key areas addressed during neurological rehabilitation include:

    • Motor skill re-education and limb strengthening
    • Balance, coordination, and posture realignment
    • Speech, swallowing, and cognitive therapy
    • Adaptive techniques for daily activities
    • Spasticity and muscle tone management

How Asthra Supports Recovery

  • Our experienced clinical team utilizes structured neuromuscular techniques and supportive monitoring to stimulate neuroplasticity and help seniors regain their highest level of autonomy.


Geriatric Mobility & Fall Prevention

  • Age-related muscle loss, balance disorders, and chronic arthritis can severely compromise daily mobility and increase the risk of dangerous falls.
  • Key Benefits of Our Program:
    • Targeted core and lower-body resistance training
    • Static and dynamic balance improvement exercises
    • Assistive device assessment, fitting, and training (walkers, canes)
    • Environmental hazard awareness and safe movement strategies
    • Substantial reduction in hospital readmission risks

What Asthra Rehabilitation Care Provides:

Recovery timelines vary depending on each individual’s clinical condition. Asthra provides tailored daily protocols to support steady, measurable progress:

  • Dedicated sessions with qualified physiotherapists and occupational specialists
  • 24/7 nursing oversight and medication administration
  • Personalized nutrition and dietary plans designed to accelerate muscle rebuilding and tissue healing



Cardiopulmonary & Functional Conditioning

  • Patients recovering from cardiac events, chronic respiratory conditions (COPD), or prolonged bed rest often suffer from severe deconditioning and fatigue.

Restoring Everyday Vitality

  • Asthra delivers physician-guided endurance protocols, monitored breathing exercises, and safe progressive movement routines designed to safely restore lung capacity, cardiac efficiency, and functional independence.

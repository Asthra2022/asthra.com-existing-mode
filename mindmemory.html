Mind & Memory Care Services in Chennai

Holistic Dementia & Memory Support at Asthra

Living with memory loss, Alzheimer's disease, or age-related dementia requires an environment built on patience, structure, and specialized clinical care. At Asthra Senior Living, our Mind & Memory Care program is purposefully designed to reduce confusion, foster emotional stability, and enhance everyday cognitive engagement.

Our dedicated memory caregivers recognize the unique challenges of progressive memory decline. We curate personalized daily routines, therapeutic cognitive exercises, and secure spaces where residents retain their dignity, independence, and peace of mind.

Our Memory Care Pillars Include:

  • Cognitive & Sensory Stimulation Therapies
  • Structured Daily Routines for Anxiety Reduction
  • 24/7 Monitored Safe and Secure Living Environments
  • Specialized Dementia & Alzheimer's Nursing
  • Nutritional Support and Assisted Daily Living (ADLs)


Specialized Alzheimer's & Dementia Care

Caring for an aging parent with advanced memory impairment requires 24/7 vigilance and empathetic handling. Asthra provides a secure setting that mitigates wandering, agitation, and sundowning symptoms. Our trained multidisciplinary staff balances clinical medication management with sensory relaxation techniques, ensuring seniors receive round-the-clock comfort in a home-like atmosphere.



Comprehensive Cognitive Wellness Programs

  • Our memory care philosophy is rooted in validating each senior's emotions while sustaining their cognitive strengths through specialized daily activities:

    • Reminiscence therapy and guided memory recall exercises
    • Low-stress, structured daily schedule to reduce disorientation
    • Compassionate personal grooming, dressing, and hygiene support
    • Continuous vital sign and psychiatric health monitoring
    • Secure indoor zones preventing unsupervised wandering
    • Brain stimulation through art, music, and gentle mobility games
    • Balanced, memory-boosting nutrition and hydration oversight
    • Regular family counseling and transparent progress updates

Why Choose Asthra's Memory Care Program?

  • We combine clinical nursing excellence with genuine empathy, ensuring seniors with cognitive challenges experience meaningful connections, safety, and an enriching lifestyle every day.


Residential Memory Living Facility

  • Transitioning into a dedicated memory care residence ensures your loved ones receive targeted interventions that standard home environments cannot always provide. Asthra Senior Living Home offers a clutter-free, accessible environment tailored to minimize triggers and reduce confusion.
  • Key Benefits for Families:
    • Guaranteed 24-hour safety and security against wandering
    • Relief from caregiver burnout with dedicated professional nursing
    • Custom dietary plans designed for cognitive and metabolic health
    • Continuous monitoring of behavioural and emotional patterns
    • A dignified, social setting where seniors never feel isolated

What Services Are Included in Memory Care?

Whether your loved one is navigating early-stage mild cognitive impairment (MCI) or requires advanced Alzheimer's support, Asthra adapts care plans to fit their specific needs:

  • 12–24 hour dedicated nursing care by dementia-trained caregivers
  • Accurate medication distribution and prescription management
  • Assistance with all Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) including bathing and feeding
  • Sensory stimulation, therapeutic music, and light physiotherapy routines
  • Emergency medical preparedness with on-call physicians
  • Safe, supervised indoor and outdoor movement spaces



Continuous Cognitive & Physical Health Monitoring

  • Memory conditions often co-exist with physical health changes. Our clinical team tracks both cognitive benchmarks and day-to-day physical metrics, identifying potential health shifts early to prevent hospital readmissions and unnecessary complications.

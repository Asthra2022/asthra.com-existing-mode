Transition Care Services in Chennai

Seamless Hospital-to-Home Transition Care

Transition Care bridges the critical gap between inpatient hospital discharge and a safe return to independent living or long-term home care. It provides structured, short-term skilled medical support designed to prevent hospital readmissions and promote complete recovery.

At Asthra Senior Living Home, our transition care services in Chennai ensure that seniors receive tailored clinical oversight, rehabilitative therapy, and continuous personal assistance during their vital recovery window.

Whether recovering from acute illnesses, joint replacement, cardiac events, or major surgeries, our multidisciplinary team coordinates every phase of care—from medication administration and vitals monitoring to mobility training and nutritional therapy.

Choose Asthra Senior Living Home for reliable, compassionate transitional care that gives family members peace of mind and empowers seniors to regain their strength safely.

Our Transition Care Services Include:

  • Post-Hospitalization Step-Down Care
  • Post-Surgical Recovery & Wound Management
  • Stroke & Neurological Rehabilitation Support
  • Orthopedic & Joint Replacement Recovery
  • Medication Reconciliation & Health Monitoring


Post-Hospitalization Step-Down Care

  • Moving straight home after an intense hospital stay can often lead to complications or readmission if adequate medical infrastructure is lacking.
  • Asthra provides a secure, clinically monitored step-down environment where seniors stabilize, rebuild routine strength, and receive continuous observation before fully resuming independent routines.


Post-Surgical Recovery & Wound Management

  • Proper post-operative dressing, infection prevention, pain control, and drain/catheter maintenance are vital for effective surgical healing.
  • Our certified nursing team ensures meticulous surgical wound care, timely pain management, and direct implementation of the discharge protocol outlined by your surgeon.


Orthopedic & Mobility Rehabilitation

  • Following hip, knee, or spine surgeries, targeted physical therapy is essential to restore balance, flexibility, and independent movement.
  • Benefits of our targeted physical therapy:
    • Customized daily physiotherapy and gait training sessions
    • Fall-risk reduction through supervised mobility assistance
    • Gradual, safe return to daily activities and functional independence


Medication Reconciliation & Continuous Vitals Monitoring

  • Hospital discharge often involves major changes in prescription routines, dosage timings, and dietary adjustments.
  • Our experienced clinical team oversees regular vital tracking, blood glucose control, oxygen therapy support, and accurate medication delivery to prevent adverse drug events.


Why Choose Asthra for Transition Care?

Every recovery journey is unique. Asthra Senior Living Home creates custom step-down recovery plans aligned with your doctor's recommendations, complete with:

  • 24/7 dedicated nursing and bedside care assistance
  • On-call doctor consultations and dedicated physiotherapy sessions
  • Nutritious, recovery-tailored dietary management
  • Comfortable, senior-friendly living suites designed for safe recovery
